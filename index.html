shoot 'em up

turn any webpage into an element-destroying arcade mini-game.

installation

drag this link directly onto your browser's bookmarks bar:

🎯 shoot 'em up

how to play

  1. Show your Bookmarks Bar (Ctrl+Shift+B or Cmd+Shift+B).
  2. Drag the 🎯 shoot 'em up link into your bookmarks bar.
  3. Visit any website and click the bookmarklet to start.
  4. Aim: Hover over elements to target them.
  5. Shoot: Left-click to fire.
  6. Dodge: Avoid red incoming bullets fired by active elements!

shooter enemies

Every 1.5 seconds, a random element from the elements given below fires red bullets at your cursor:

  • <button> (Buttons)
  • <img> (Images)
  • <input> (Form Inputs)
  • <video> (Videos)
  • <canvas> (Canvas Elements)

destructible elements & HP

  • 100 HP: <h1>, <h2>, <h3>, <img>, <button>
  • 50 HP: <video>, <canvas>
  • 20 HP: <p>, <span>, <a>, <li>, <input>

immune elements

These elements cannot be shot or target you:

  • Game HUD & Target HP bar
  • Active Bullets
  • Game Over / Victory Modals
  • Main Page Containers (<body>, <html>)
