🎯 shoot 'em up 🎯

a tiny bookmarklet game that turns any website into a shooter game

setup:

first, turn on your browser's bookmark bar (if it's hidden then press Ctrl+Shift+B or Cmd+Shift+B).

Then drag this button right into your bookmarks bar:

shoot 'em up (DRAG ME!)

if you just want to try this out before setting it up, then click on the button given above and the game should start

how to play

  • go to a random website.
  • click on the "shoot 'em up" link on your bookmarks bar.
  • move your mouse around to aim at elements.
  • left click to shoot them!
  • watch out for the red bullets fired back at your cursor and dodge them!

demo

shooooter enemy

every 1.5 seconds, one of these elements will try to shoot red bullets at you:

  • button tags
  • img tags
  • input boxes
  • video players
  • canvas elements

shooters example:

this section won't make sense until you play the game on this site :P

element health points (HP)

100 HP (hard to kill): h1, h2, h3, img, button

50 HP (medium): video, canvas

20 HP (easy): p, span, a, li, input

immune elements:

they can't be destroyed:

  • the game HUD and HP bars
  • flying bullets
  • game over and victory popups
  • background containers like body and html
